AI-Powered
Invoice Automation

An intelligent automation solution developed for Grupo Dinosaurio to transform a manual invoice-processing workflow into an automated, AI-powered data extraction pipeline.

01 — Overview

The project

The objective was to automate the extraction and preparation of invoice data, reducing manual data entry and creating a more reliable and scalable process for the company.

⚡

Automation

Automated the workflow from invoice reception to database-ready records.

🧠

Artificial Intelligence

Used Google Gemini to extract structured information from invoice documents.

🔗

Data Integration

Transformed extracted information into structured records ready for SQL integration.

02 — Challenge

The problem

Manual data entry

Every incoming invoice had to be manually reviewed and entered into the company's internal system. Receptionists were responsible for manually loading the information and individual items contained in each invoice.

What needed to change

The process needed to become faster, more consistent and less dependent on repetitive manual work, while maintaining a high level of accuracy in the extracted information.

03 — Solution

04 — Development Process

Built through testing and iteration

One of the most important parts of the project was determining which AI model could provide the most reliable invoice extraction. The solution was not implemented in a single step: it was tested, evaluated and continuously improved.

01

AI Model Evaluation

OpenAI, Google Gemini and Claude were evaluated using real invoice examples to compare their extraction capabilities.

02

Model Selection

After several weeks of testing, Google Gemini was selected as the model that best matched the requirements of the project.

03

Structured Data Extraction

The AI was instructed to extract the required invoice information and return it in a predefined JSON structure.

04

Prompt Engineering & Validation

Extracted data was loaded into Monday.com for manual verification. The results were analyzed and the prompt was continuously refined to improve accuracy.

05

Process Stabilization

Once the extraction process reached a stable level of approximately 97% effectiveness, the validation workflow was incorporated into the final automation.

06

Database Integration

The extracted information was validated, checked for duplicates and transformed into records ready to be inserted into the SQL environment.
