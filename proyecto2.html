01 — Overview

The project

The goal was to automate the process of scheduling introductory meetings with potential clients. The assistant could understand incoming emails, check a salesperson's availability, suggest suitable meeting times, handle alternative requests and finally schedule the meeting once the client confirmed their preferred option.

🤖

AI Agent

An intelligent agent powered by GPT-4o was responsible for understanding emails and coordinating the scheduling process.

📅

Smart Scheduling

🔗

Multi-Scenario Architecture

02 — Challenge

The problem

Manual scheduling

Coordinating meetings with potential clients required communication between the client and the sales team to identify available time slots and manually schedule the meeting.

What needed to change

The process needed to become more autonomous while still respecting the salesperson's calendar, internal meeting rules and the specific requirements of the scheduling process.

03 — Solution

An AI agent for meeting coordination

The final solution transformed the incoming email into an automated scheduling workflow. The AI agent interpreted the request, interacted with different Make scenarios and managed the conversation until the meeting was successfully scheduled.

04 — Architecture

Four scenarios working together

Instead of placing the entire scheduling logic into a single workflow, the solution was divided into four specialized Make scenarios. The AI agent orchestrated these scenarios depending on the state of the conversation.

SCENARIO 01

Availability Check

Checked the salesperson's calendar to identify available time slots for the requested meeting date.

SCENARIO 02

Internal Meetings

Evaluated internal meetings and determined whether a slot could be used according to the company's internal rules. Meetings associated with the relevant area could be used based on the email domain information.

SCENARIO 03

Meeting Scheduling

Once the client selected one of the proposed options, this scenario created the 30-minute meeting in the salesperson's calendar.

SCENARIO 04

Data Storage

Stored relevant information from the interaction and scheduled meeting in a database for record keeping and future reference.

05 — AI Agent

Designed around clear rules

The agent was designed with explicit instructions defining what it could do, what information it could access and how it should behave during a scheduling conversation.

INPUT: Incoming client email
ANALYZE: Identify whether the email contains a meeting request
CHECK: Salesperson calendar availability
FILTER: Apply internal meeting rules
PROPOSE: Three available 30-minute time slots
WAIT: Client confirmation or alternative request
BOOK: Create the meeting after confirmation
STORE: Save relevant meeting information
06 — Agent Behavior

Rules and constraints

The prompt defined the boundaries of the assistant and helped ensure that the agent followed consistent behavior throughout the conversation.

Only handle questions and requests related to meetings.
Always propose three available meeting options.
Meetings have a duration of 30 minutes.
Saturdays and Sundays are excluded from scheduling.
Use Argentina's time zone when scheduling meetings.
Never invent availability or personal information.
Offer alternatives when the requested time is unavailable.
Create the meeting only after client confirmation.
